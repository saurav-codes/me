The story so far

I taught myself to code on freeCodeCamp, then spent five years shipping Django backends for clients around the world: payroll SaaS, recruiting tools, lead routing, voice AI. Remote gigs, full-time and freelance.

Between jobs I took breaks on purpose, and I used them to build my own projects. LazyPlanner (AI planning SaaS), ox (deploy tool), Tymr (focus timer), plus plenty of ideas that never left the notebook. Those breaks were also school of a different kind: Go, HTMX, real-time AI, what makes design good, and sales (cold email, copywriting, reading buyers).

Now I am trying to make my own indie products work, LazyPlanner first, while keeping a foot in client work when I want it.
